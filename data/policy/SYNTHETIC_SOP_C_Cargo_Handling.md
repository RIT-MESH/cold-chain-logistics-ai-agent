# Southern California Logistics Operations — SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION SOP
## Standard Operating Procedure (SYNTHETIC): Shipment and Cargo Handling
**Version:** 1.0-demo | **Effective Date:** Jan 2021 (demo context) | **Status:** SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT — NOT AN APPROVED OPERATIONAL POLICY | **Category:** C. Shipment and Cargo Handling | **Source:** synthetic-demo | **Access:** internal-demo

### C1. Loading and unloading
* Load refrigerated cargo only after pre-cooling the container to the configured range.
* Keep door-open time during loading/unloading to the minimum practical; record door events.
* Do not stack cargo in a way that blocks the airflow sensor.

### C2. Storage procedures
* Maintain the storage environment within the cargo's configured range before loading.
* Record storage temperature at handover; any out-of-range storage reading is documented and attached to the shipment record.

### C3. Cargo handling restrictions
* Temperature-controlled cargo is never left on an unpowered vehicle during a break.
* If the cooling unit fails mid-route, apply SOP A3 corrective actions and document the failure.

### C4. Temperature-controlled transportation
* The cooling unit runs continuously from loading to unloading; drivers verify operation at each stop.
* Any planned route deviation longer than 1 hour is approved by the duty supervisor and recorded on the shipment.
