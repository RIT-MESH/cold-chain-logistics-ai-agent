# Southern California Logistics Operations — SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION SOP
## Standard Operating Procedure (SYNTHETIC): Chain of Custody
**Version:** 1.0-demo | **Effective Date:** Jan 2021 (demo context) | **Status:** SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT — NOT AN APPROVED OPERATIONAL POLICY | **Category:** D. Chain of Custody | **Source:** synthetic-demo | **Access:** internal-demo

### D1. Custody transfers
* Every custody transfer is recorded with a handoff number, the receiving party, the handover time, and the seal status.
* Custody is never transferred without a recorded handoff entry.

### D2. Shipment handover verification
1. Before accepting a shipment, the receiver verifies the seal integrity and the handoff record.
2. A broken or missing seal is recorded on the handoff and escalated to the duty supervisor.
3. The receiver confirms the temperature record covers the full transit period without unexplained gaps.

### D3. Delivery acceptance
* The receiver records an acceptance verdict (accepted / accepted with note / rejected) with the shipment.
* Partial or conditional acceptance must state the reason and reference the evidence relied on.

### D4. Exception documentation
* Any anomaly observed at handover (temperature gap, seal exception, delay) is documented on the handoff record and referenced in the shipment's exception notes.
* Exceptions are never resolved verbally — a written record is required for every case.
