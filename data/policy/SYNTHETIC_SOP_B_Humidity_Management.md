# Southern California Logistics Operations — SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION SOP
## Standard Operating Procedure (SYNTHETIC): Humidity Management
**Version:** 1.0-demo | **Effective Date:** Jan 2021 (demo context) | **Status:** SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT — NOT AN APPROVED OPERATIONAL POLICY | **Category:** B. Humidity Management | **Source:** synthetic-demo | **Access:** internal-demo

### B1. Approved humidity limits
The demonstration dataset records humidity as a monitored environmental value. Cargo-specific humidity limits are set per shipment when applicable; where no limit is configured, humidity readings are monitored for trend only and no deviation action applies.
* No universal humidity limit is defined by this SOP — always check the shipment record first.

### B2. Humidity deviation handling
1. When a configured humidity limit is exceeded, verify sensor operation (see SOP F1).
2. Adjust the container ventilation/dehumidification settings via the driver.
3. Record the deviation and any corrective action with UTC timestamps.
4. Escalate to the duty supervisor if the deviation persists after 1 hour.

### B3. Environmental monitoring procedure
* Review humidity alongside temperature: simultaneous deviations increase urgency.
* Do not infer cargo damage from humidity alone — record the observation and the corrective action taken.
